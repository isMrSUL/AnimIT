# Code of Conduct

AnimIT is a friendly community project for wrestling-game modders. Everyone is welcome, whatever their
experience, language or background.

**Please:**
- be respectful and patient — many people here are learning, and many don't speak English as a first language;
- give helpful, specific feedback on code and ideas, not on people;
- credit other people's work (tools, research, mods) when you use it.

**Not allowed:**
- insults, harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks;
- sharing game files, pirated games or proprietary files (such as Oodle DLLs) in issues, discussions or pull
  requests;
- posting other people's private information.

Problems can be reported to the maintainer, **SUL** ([@isMrSUL](https://github.com/isMrSUL)), privately.
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these
rules.
